# Code of conduct

Everyone who contributes, reports an issue, or joins a project discussion is
expected to help keep the community useful and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect people's privacy, time, identity, and experience.
- Obtain permission before sharing someone else's personal information.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Repeated
personal attacks or disruption after a request to stop are also unacceptable.

## Report and moderate conduct

Use GitHub's abuse-reporting controls for abusive platform content. For project
moderation, contact [@dafzthomas](https://github.com/dafzthomas) through a contact
method published on that profile. Keep sensitive personal details out of public
issues. Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private security channel](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, reject a contribution,
or restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior. Where
appropriate, maintainers explain the decision and give the person an opportunity
to correct their behavior. Threats or serious harassment can result in immediate
removal. Maintainers follow the same standards.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. It is a project-specific policy, not an adoption of an external conduct
or enforcement program.
